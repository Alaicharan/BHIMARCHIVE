1920s

Public Leadership

His writings, journalism and social movements became important parts of his public work.

1947

Constitutional Role

1950

How the platform works

1. Historical documents are digitized using OCR.

2. Metadata is attached to every digital object.

3. AI embeddings enable semantic retrieval.

4. Knowledge graphs connect related concepts.

5. The AI assistant retrieves relevant archival sources.

6. Users receive answers together with source references.
